Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2, 2025

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the Google Map Contact Extractor Chrome extension ("the Extension") handles user data. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about our data practices.

Information Collection and Use

The Extension is designed to help users efficiently find and extract contact information from businesses using the Google Maps API. Here's what you should know about our data practices:

  • User-provided Information: The Extension stores your preferences (such as default location and search radius) and search history locally on your device using Chrome's storage API. This information never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any external servers.
  • Business Information: When you perform a search, the Extension retrieves business information (names, addresses, phone numbers) from the Google Maps API. This information is displayed to you and can be exported in various formats.
  • API Interactions: The Extension communicates directly with Google Maps API servers to retrieve business data based on your search criteria.

Data Storage

All data stored by the Extension is kept locally on your device:

  • Your preferences and settings
  • Your search history
  • Exported business contact information

None of this information is transmitted to our servers or any third-party servers except Google Maps API for the purpose of retrieving business information.

Google Maps API

The Extension uses the Google Maps API to search for and retrieve business information. Your interactions with this service are subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

When you perform a search, the following information is sent to Google:

  • Search location (address or coordinates)
  • Search radius
  • Business type

Permissions

The Extension requests the following permissions:

  • activeTab: Required for the extension's popup interface to display and function correctly.
  • scripting: Required to execute JavaScript code that powers the search functionality and processes API responses.
  • host permissions (maps.googleapis.com): Required to access Google Maps API endpoints to retrieve business information.
  • storage: Required to save your preferences and search history locally on your device.

Data Sharing

The Extension does not share any user data with third parties. The only external service the Extension communicates with is the Google Maps API, and this is solely for the purpose of retrieving business information based on your search criteria.

Children's Privacy

The Extension is not intended for use by children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your Rights

Since all user data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control over this information:

  • You can view and modify your preferences at any time through the Extension's settings.
  • You can clear your search history through the Extension's interface.
  • You can uninstall the Extension at any time, which will remove all stored data from your device.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
